About Coupons, Deals & Steals

Who We Are

Coupons, Deals & Steals is a free deal alert service that uses AI to monitor Amazon 24/7 and surface the biggest price drops across every product category.

We post to our Telegram channel the moment we find a deal at 30% off or more — so you never have to spend time searching for discounts.

How It Works

Our system scans Amazon continuously, scoring each deal based on discount percentage, product rating, and category demand. Only the best deals make it through.

When a qualifying deal is detected, our AI writes a full description and SEO page so you can find deals through Google too — not just our channel.

We cover electronics, kitchen appliances, clothing, toys, home goods, beauty, sports equipment, books, and more. If Amazon discounts it by 30% or more, we find it.
